# RR IPL 2027 auction: roster scenarios (checked 7 Nov 2026)

## What is confirmed

The last published Rajasthan Royals squad page is for IPL 2026. In the 2026 trade cycle RR sent Sanju Samson to CSK for Ravindra Jadeja and Sam Curran; Nitish Rana moved to DC and Donovan Ferreira came to RR. RR’s final 2026 squad and replacement names are not a confirmed 2027 retention list. The 2027 retention list is not yet published.

On 6 Nov Cricbuzz reported a possible three or four player RR–LSG trade: RR were pursuing an LSG pace bowler with a successful previous season, an LSG spinner might also be involved, and RR could send one or two batters/all-rounders. The report says the deal had not gone through and did not name the players. It is a rumour with role clues, not confirmed movement. We therefore do not name an incoming player.

## Conditional auction shortlist types

### Scenario A — RR–LSG trade completes with an incoming pacer and perhaps spinner

1. **Young uncapped Indian middle-order finisher** who can attack spin in overs 7–15 and sustain a high strike rate at the death. RR would be giving up a batter; the exact player/slot is unknown, and the 2026 roster already has strong openers.
2. **Batting all-rounder** only if RR send an all-rounder and do not receive equivalent batting/bowling coverage. Prioritize at least two credible skills, not a one-format label.
3. **Opener only if the outgoing batter is an opener.** The model should branch on official names once released; do not assume Yashasvi Jaiswal is included.
4. Reduce generic pace/spin demand if the incoming LSG bowlers are contracted, available and role-compatible. Keep an injury/workload contingency only after availability verification.

### Scenario B — no RR–LSG trade

1. **Young uncapped Indian powerplay bowler with control and wicket threat.** RR’s powerplay runs conceded rose from 9.41 to 10.43 per six legal balls, ranking 4th-worst in 2026. Screen economy, dot-ball rate and wickets together; use death bowling as a secondary need because that phase improved from 12.71 to 11.06.
2. **Indian middle-order/finishing depth** if 2027 retentions leave a bench gap; screen for spin-hitting and role-specific evidence.
3. **Spin cover** after the retention list; the 2026 squad already has Ravi Bishnoi and Jadeja, so do not elevate an extra spinner without a departure or clear matchup requirement.
