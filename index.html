How the model scores

Transparent scouting rubric · role-relevant evidence and conditional squad need

The shortlist is built for this competition: explicit uncapped eligibility is a gate; there is no invented age cutoff, and younger eligible players receive a higher age score. Ranking uses role-specific domestic T20 performance (60%), age (25%) and a user-selected, provisional RR needs scenario (15%). Batting evidence uses strike rate, average, runs per match and sample depth; bowling evidence uses wicket rate, economy, dot-ball rate and sample depth. All measures are compared within the eligible role pool, not against an absolute benchmark; scores are relative to the uploaded candidates and may change when the pool changes. Small samples are flagged. Historical IPL appearance models remain visible only as context; they do not decide the top 10. The official pack declaration is only for missing status and never overrides an explicit capped label. Public rows join only through one unique verified name/alias.
