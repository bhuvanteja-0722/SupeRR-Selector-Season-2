How the model scores

Two-stage supervised baseline · season-held-out validation

Logistic regression estimates next-season IPL appearance. Ridge regression estimates per-match impact among players who appeared, labelled as (runs + 20 × wickets) per IPL match. Expected impact is appearance probability × predicted impact. The final 100-point shortlist blends expected-impact percentile (70%), youth percentile (20%) and provisional RR role fit (10%).
